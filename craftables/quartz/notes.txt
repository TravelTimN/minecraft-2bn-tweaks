2BN-Tweaks | Craftables | Quartz


=== INFO ===

Craftable raw Nether Quartz: 4x Netherrack, 4x Basalt, 1x Magma Block -> 8x Nether Quartz.
Balanced for mid-game Nether survival: magma blocks are cheap to reach, and basalt exists in abundance in basalt deltas.
